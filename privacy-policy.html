Unsubinator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version. Unsubinator reads only the Gmail header fields it needs to find newsletter senders and their published unsubscribe instructions. Everything happens inside your browser. We don't receive, sell or analyse your email data.

1. Who we are

Unsubinator is made by Look Behind You Software, a small company based in Scotland. Contact: hello@lbysoftware.co.uk.

2. What the extension accesses

With your permission, Unsubinator uses the Gmail API's read-only scope. For messages matching the options you choose (mail category, time range, and any domains you exclude), it requests message identifiers and these header fields: From, List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results (so it can check that Gmail verified the sender's signature). It does not request message bodies, attachments, contacts, drafts or sent mail.

3. How that data is used

  • Messages are grouped by sender, and the unsubscribe method each sender published is identified.
  • Results are kept in Chrome's session storage so they survive the popup closing. They are cleared when the browser session ends or when you disconnect.
  • Your scan settings (mail category, time range and excluded domains) are saved on your computer in Chrome's extension storage so they are remembered next time. They are never sent anywhere.

4. What leaves your browser

  • Gmail API requests go directly from the extension to Google over HTTPS.
  • If you select a standards-based one-click unsubscribe, the extension asks your browser for temporary access to that sender's website, sends the prescribed request directly to the HTTPS address the sender published, and then gives that access back. These requests are only sent when Gmail reports that the sender's DKIM signature passed.
  • Ordinary confirmation links and email unsubscribe requests are never sent automatically.

5. What we collect

Nothing. Look Behind You Software runs no Unsubinator analytics or data server. We don't receive or keep email addresses, message details, sign-in tokens, search choices, unsubscribe choices or browsing activity. The extension contains no advertising or tracking code.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Unsubinator's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the unsubscribe features you see in the extension. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold or transferred to anyone, and no human reads it.

7. Control and deletion

  • Choosing Disconnect in the extension revokes Unsubinator's access with Google, removes its cached sign-in token and clears the session results.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored on your computer.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the extension's disclosures before any new data practice begins.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: hello@lbysoftware.co.uk.
