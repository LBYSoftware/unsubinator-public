Unsubinator for Gmail

In development

Old newsletters, meet your exit. Unsubinator is a Chrome extension that shows you who keeps filling your Gmail, lets you choose who can leave, and sends proper one-click unsubscribe requests in a tidy batch.

What it does

  • Scans only the sender and unsubscribe details of your emails. Never message bodies.
  • Choose the mail category and how far back to look, and list any domains it must never touch.
  • Only offers one-click unsubscribe when Gmail confirms the sender's signature checked out.
  • Doesn't delete, archive, move or label anything in your mailbox.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sent to us.

Status

Unsubinator is still being built and tested, and isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. We'll update this page when it is.
